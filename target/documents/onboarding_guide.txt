Getting Started Guide

Welcome! Here's how to set up your account in under 5 minutes.

Step 1: Create your workspace
After signing up, you'll be prompted to name your workspace. This is
where all your team's projects will live.

Step 2: Invite your team
Go to Settings > Team > Invite Members. You can invite up to 5 people
on the Free plan, or unlimited on Pro and Enterprise.

Step 3: Create your first project
Click "New Project" on the dashboard. Choose a template or start blank.

Step 4: Connect integrations
We support Slack, Google Drive, and GitHub integrations. Find these
under Settings > Integrations.

Need help? Our support team responds within 24 hours on weekdays.
