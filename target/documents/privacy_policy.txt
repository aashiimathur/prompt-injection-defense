Privacy Policy

We collect only the information necessary to provide our services: your
name, email address, and billing details when you sign up for a paid plan.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Account data is retained for as
long as your account is active, and deleted within 90 days of account
closure upon request.

You can request a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted at any
time by emailing privacy@example.com.

We use cookies only for essential session management, not for tracking
or advertising purposes.
