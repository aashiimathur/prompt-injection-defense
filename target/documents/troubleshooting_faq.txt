Troubleshooting Guide

Problem: I can't log in.
Solution: Try resetting your password via the "Forgot Password" link.
If you still can't log in after resetting, check that you're using the
correct email address associated with your account.

Problem: My changes aren't saving.
Solution: This is usually caused by an unstable internet connection.
Check your connection and try refreshing the page. Unsaved changes are
cached locally for up to 10 minutes.

Problem: The app is running slowly.
Solution: Clear your browser cache, or try using an incognito/private
window to rule out browser extension conflicts.

Problem: I'm not receiving email notifications.
Solution: Check your spam folder, and make sure notifications are
enabled under Settings > Notifications. Some corporate email filters
also block automated emails — check with your IT department.

Still stuck? Contact support@example.com with a description of the
issue and any error messages you saw.
