Pricing Details

Free Plan — $0/month
- Up to 5 team members
- 3 active projects
- Community support only

Pro Plan — $8/user/month
- Unlimited team members
- Unlimited projects
- Priority email support
- Advanced integrations (Slack, GitHub, Google Drive)

Enterprise Plan — Custom pricing
- Everything in Pro
- Single Sign-On (SSO)
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom SLA and onboarding support
- Volume discounts available

All paid plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to
start the trial. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from
Settings > Billing.

Annual billing gets a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.
